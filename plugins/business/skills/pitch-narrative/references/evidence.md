# Evidence table

Keep one evidence table for the pitch. Add a row only when the approved plan already makes that claim.

| Claim | Kind | Source | Mark |
|-------|------|--------|------|
| The sentence as it will appear in the pitch, copied from the plan | the plan's `fact` or `assumption` | the plan's URL, document, or confirmation line | the plan's blank, `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`, or `[DECISION NEEDED]` |

Copy kind, source, and mark from the plan. Do not upgrade an assumption to a fact. Do not drop a mark to shorten the page.

Never invent market size or finance. If the plan left TAM, price, or cash blank or marked, the pitch leaves that same blank or mark.

Put the evidence table in the pitch so a reader can see which sentences came from the plan and which gaps remain.
