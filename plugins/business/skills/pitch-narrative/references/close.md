# Close

End the pitch with three lists copied from the approved plan. Do not add a new weakness, decision, or action.

## Weaknesses

Copy the plan's gaps that would change a yes: missing buyers, untested price, thin competition notes, or a cash question with no confirmed figure.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` the plan still carries. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Copy the plan's next actions. If the plan has none, mark `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` and ask the founder to add them on the plan first.
