# Story order

Write a short investor or lender story in this order. Use only sentences the approved plan already supports.

1. Offer and buyer
2. Why this buyer pays now
3. Competitive position, including the status-quo alternative already named in the plan
4. Year-one goal and the cash question the plan already asks
5. What the founder is asking the reader for, when the plan already states an ask

For a lender, keep the cash question and repayment or collateral lines the plan already has, and leave them marked when the plan left them marked. For an investor, keep the year-one goal and the open decisions the plan already lists.

Write the pitch after you have the approved file open. Do not introduce a claim that is missing from that file. If a spine step has no supporting sentence in the plan, write the step as a gap and keep the plan's mark.
