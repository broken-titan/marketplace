# Intake

Ask for these before any pitch draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Approved plan | Path to the finished plan the founder has already accepted |
| Reader | Investor or lender, and any length limit the founder already set |

Do not draft from a chat thread, a slide, or a folder of notes. Ask for the approved file. Stop this skill when the founder cannot point to one.

Copy the plan's offer, buyer, and year-one goal as they stand. Do not fill a field from a guess, and do not add a number the plan does not already carry.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
