# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the price if they stay open: an unchosen model, a band with no source, a missing comp, or a recommended number the founder has not locked.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: ask one buyer what they would pay, open a named pricing page, or lock the model. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
