# Model, bands, comps, and rationale

Write these blocks in order.

## Model

Choose one: one-time, subscription, or hybrid. Write what the buyer pays for (license, seat, job, month) and when cash arrives. Mark `[DECISION NEEDED]` when the founder has not chosen.

## Bands

List a low, middle, and high published or confirmed price the founder would consider, with the unit on each row. A band that is only a working guess is an assumption. A band with no source stays blank or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.

## Comps

Name public prices for direct rivals or close substitutes. Each comp needs a URL, a retrieval date, and the unit. Recheck the page on the day of the draft. Skip a private quote unless the founder confirmed it and you can date that confirmation.

## Rationale

State why the chosen or candidate price fits the offer, the buyer, and the comps that exist. Point at the evidence rows. If the founder has not chosen a price, write the rationale as a comparison and mark the recommended number `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

Ask these before treating any number as real.

1. What has a customer already paid, or what have they said they would pay?
2. Which competitor page is the founder willing to sit next to?
3. Who confirms the number: the founder, an advisor, or a signed quote?
