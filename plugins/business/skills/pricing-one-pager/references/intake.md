# Intake

Ask for these before any pricing draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Offer | What a buyer gets and what they pay with |
| Buyer | Who already feels the problem and can approve a buy |
| Chosen price | Any number a customer has paid, promised, or the founder has already locked |

Copy a model preference (one-time, subscription, hybrid) when the founder already stated it. Mark the model `[DECISION NEEDED]` when they have not.

Do not fill a field from a guess. If the founder names a person, a company, or a number, copy it as a fact and put it on the evidence table. If they describe a hope, label it an assumption.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
