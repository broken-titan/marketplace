# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the size story if they stay open: a missing geography, an unsourced TAM, a segment with no buyer proof, or a trend with no date.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: pull a dated census or registry extract, confirm the geography, or sit with an advisor on the SAM cut. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
