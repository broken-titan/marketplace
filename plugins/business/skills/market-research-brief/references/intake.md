# Intake

Ask for these two before any sizing draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Offer | What a buyer gets and what they pay with (money, time, data, or a contract) |
| Geography | Countries, regions, or cities the founder will sell in during the period under study |

Copy a buyer note, a stage, or a constraint when the founder already stated it. Mark a missing extras field only when a later section needs it.

Do not fill a field from a guess. If the founder names a person, a company, a place, or a number, copy it as a fact and put it on the evidence table. If they describe a hope, label it an assumption.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
