# Evidence table

Keep one evidence table for the brief. Add a row when a section makes a material claim.

| Claim | Kind | Source | Mark |
|-------|------|--------|------|
| The sentence as it will appear in the brief | `fact` or `assumption` | URL, document, or "founder confirmed <date>" | blank, `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`, or `[DECISION NEEDED]` |

Kind is `fact` only when a source exists or the founder / an advisor confirmed the figure. Kind is `assumption` when the sentence is a working guess.

Use `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when the claim needs a source and none exists. Use `[DECISION NEEDED]` when the founder still has to choose the offer or the geography.

Never invent market size or other statistics. A TAM, SAM, SOM, growth rate, spend figure, or buyer count with no source stays blank or marked; do not supply a placeholder number.

Put the evidence table in the brief so a reader can see facts, assumptions, and gaps in one place.
