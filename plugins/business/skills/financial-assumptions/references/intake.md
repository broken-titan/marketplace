# Intake

Ask for these before any driver draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Offer | What a buyer gets and what they pay with |
| Stage | Idea, pre-revenue, first customers, or expanding a live offer |
| Confirmed figures | Any price, volume, cost, or opex a customer, invoice, or advisor already locked |

Copy cash-timing notes (deposit, invoice lag, prepaid, monthly) when the founder already stated them.

Do not fill a field from a guess. If the founder names a person, a company, or a number, copy it as a fact, mark it `observed`, and put it on the evidence table. If they describe a hope, label it an assumption and mark it `assumed`.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
