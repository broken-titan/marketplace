# Break-even logic

Write the condition under which year-one cash-in covers cash-out. Use the drivers from `drivers.md`. Keep the formula visible and the factors labeled.

Example shape: `break-even volume = (opex) ÷ (price − COGS per unit)` when those factors exist and the units match. Leave a factor blank or marked when it is missing.

State the cash-timing assumption next to the formula (deposit, invoice lag, prepaid, monthly). Mark `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when timing is unknown.

## Clarifying questions

Ask these before treating break-even as a decision.

1. How many days sit between a yes and cash in the account?
2. Which costs stay even if volume is zero?
3. Who confirms the spreadsheet: the founder, an accountant, or another advisor?
4. What must stay true for the year to close with cash still in the account?

Stop at the formula and these questions when the answers are missing. Do not estimate a bank balance or write a twelve-month ledger to make the section look finished.
