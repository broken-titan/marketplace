# Evidence table

Keep one evidence table for the assumptions page. Add a row when a section makes a material claim.

| Claim | Kind | Observed or assumed | Source | Mark |
|-------|------|---------------------|--------|------|
| The sentence as it will appear on the page | `fact` or `assumption` | `observed` or `assumed` | URL, document, invoice, or "founder confirmed <date>" | blank, `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`, or `[DECISION NEEDED]` |

Kind is `fact` and observed only when a source exists or the founder / an advisor confirmed the figure. Kind is `assumption` and assumed when the sentence is a working guess.

Use `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when the claim needs a source and none exists. Use `[DECISION NEEDED]` when the founder still has to choose a driver.

Never invent finance. A price, volume, COGS, opex, or cash figure with no source stays blank or marked; do not supply a placeholder number and do not build a fake ledger around it.

Put the evidence table in the assumptions page so a reader can see facts, assumptions, and gaps in one place.
