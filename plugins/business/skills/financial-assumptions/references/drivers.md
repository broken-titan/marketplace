# Year-one drivers

Build an assumption-driven structure. Do not fill cells with invented dollars.

| Driver | What to list |
|--------|----------------|
| Price | What a sale charges, the unit, and whether the figure is observed or assumed |
| Volume | How many customers, seats, or jobs year one needs, and the period |
| COGS | Cost to deliver one unit of the offer |
| Opex | Tools, people, rent, and compliance that do not move one-for-one with a single sale |

Use labels, units, and formulas. Example: `monthly revenue = paying customers × average payment`. Leave the factors blank or marked until confirmed.

A figure the founder or an advisor confirmed is observed; write the date next to it. Every other figure is assumed or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.

Ask these before treating any number as real.

1. What has a customer already paid, or what have they said they would pay?
2. How many paying customers, seats, or jobs exist today, and how many are still a plan?
3. Which delivery costs are already being spent, and which are still a plan?
4. Which tools, people, and compliance costs are already on a card or a contract?
