# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the year-one picture if they stay open: an unconfirmed price, a volume with no buyer proof, a COGS or opex line with no invoice, or a break-even factor still blank.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: confirm a cost, pull a dated invoice, or sit with an advisor on the break-even formula. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
