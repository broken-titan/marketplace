## Niche pressure-test

## Goals and value proposition

## Market overview

Recheck fast-moving inputs (pricing pages, store ranks, census extracts, ad-library spend) on the day of the draft. Leave a figure blank or marked when the source is stale or missing.

## Competition

## Year-one financial structure

## Marketing and sales

## Operations and management

## Executive summary
