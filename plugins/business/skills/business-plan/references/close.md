# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the plan if they stay open: missing buyers, untested price, thin competition notes, or a cash question with no confirmed figure.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: talk to a named buyer, confirm a cost, pull a dated market source, or sit with an advisor on the year-one structure. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
