# Intake

Ask for these five before any section draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Mission | Why the founder is building this, in one or two sentences |
| Offer | What a buyer gets and what they pay with (money, time, data, or a contract) |
| Audience | Who already feels the problem and can buy or approve a buy |
| Stage | Idea, pre-revenue, first customers, or expanding a live offer |
| Constraints | Time, cash, licenses, geography, team size, and anything the founder will not do |

Do not fill a field from a guess. If the founder names a person, a company, or a number, copy it as a fact and put it on the evidence table. If they describe a hope, label it an assumption.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
