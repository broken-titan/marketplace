# Channels and motion

Prefer channels the founder can already use. A channel that needs an unconfirmed budget stays `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

## Channels

List where a first buyer can hear about the offer. Write the access the founder already has (list, license, partner, shop, room) next to each channel. Drop a channel that needs money or a hire the founder has not chosen.

## Acquisition

Describe the first touch and the ask. Use observed replies when they exist. Mark `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when no one has been asked yet. Leave CAC blank or marked until an invoice or a confirmed spend plus a confirmed close exists.

## Conversion

Describe the conversation that turns a reply into a yes: who talks, what is sent, and what "yes" looks like. A close rate needs observed deals or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.

## Retention

Describe how a buyer stays, renews, or buys again. A retention or LTV figure needs observed renewals, a contract term the founder confirmed, or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.

Ask these before treating a rate as real.

1. Where has a buyer already come from?
2. What can the founder spend this month in cash and hours?
3. How many conversations have become a yes, and over what period?
4. How many buyers have paid a second time, and when?
