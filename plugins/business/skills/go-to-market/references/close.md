# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the motion if they stay open: a channel with no access, a budget still unmarked, a close with no observed yes, or a retention claim with no second payment.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: run one named channel they already can use, ask one buyer how they heard, or lock the monthly spend. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
