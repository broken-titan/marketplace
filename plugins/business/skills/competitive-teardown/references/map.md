# Rival map

Name buyers' real alternatives. A unique feature still sits next to a spreadsheet, a contractor, or inertia.

| Kind | Who belongs |
|------|-------------|
| Direct | Offers a buyer can swap for this offer with little change in job |
| Indirect | Offers that solve the same job another way, including adjacent products |
| Substitute | Spreadsheets, contractors, internal tools, or other workarounds |
| Status quo | Doing nothing, delaying a buy, or staying on the current fix |

Write at least one name in Direct, at least one name in Indirect or Substitute, and a Status quo line. Mark a cell `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when the founder has not looked; still write the status-quo line from what buyers do today.

Ask the founder who they lost a deal to, who they copy prices from, and what a buyer uses on a week they skip this offer. Record those names before you search.
