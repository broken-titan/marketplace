# Evidence table

Keep one evidence table for the teardown. Add a row when a section makes a material claim.

| Claim | Kind | Source | Mark |
|-------|------|--------|------|
| The sentence as it will appear in the teardown | `fact` or `assumption` | URL, document, or "founder confirmed <date>" | blank, `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`, or `[DECISION NEEDED]` |

Kind is `fact` only when a source exists or the founder / an advisor confirmed the figure. Kind is `assumption` when the sentence is a working guess.

Use `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when the claim needs a source and none exists. Use `[DECISION NEEDED]` when the founder still has to choose who is in the set or what wedge they will hold.

Never invent prices, share, or win rates. A list price, discount, conversion claim, or "no one else does this" sentence with no source stays blank or marked; do not supply a placeholder number.

Put the evidence table in the teardown so a reader can see facts, assumptions, and gaps in one place.
