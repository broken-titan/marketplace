# Close

End the document with three lists. Pull items from the evidence table and from unanswered intake fields.

## Weaknesses

Write the gaps that would change the map if they stay open: an unnamed direct rival, a price with no page, a wedge the founder has not chosen, or a status-quo line that is still blank.

## Open decisions

Copy every `[DECISION NEEDED]` that still blocks a section. One line per decision.

## Next actions

Give the founder a short list they can do next week: open a named rival's pricing page, ask one buyer what they use today, or choose the wedge. Each action should close one weakness or one open decision.
