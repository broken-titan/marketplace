# Strengths, pricing, and wedge

For each named rival, write the blocks below. Skip a block only when it is marked.

## Strengths and weaknesses

State what the rival already does well for the buyer's job, and what a buyer complains about or works around. Each sentence needs a dated source (site, review, founder call) or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`.

## Pricing and positioning

Record the public price or plan the rival shows, the unit (seat, job, month), and the promise on the page. Recheck the pricing page on the day of the draft and keep the URL plus the retrieval date. Mark `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` when the price is hidden or the page is gone.

## Wedge

State the narrow job, buyer, or constraint where this offer can win a first yes against the named set. The wedge needs a founder choice or `[DECISION NEEDED]`. Do not claim the market is empty to make the wedge look wide.

Ask for a lost-deal note or a buyer quote before you treat a joint weakness as fact.
