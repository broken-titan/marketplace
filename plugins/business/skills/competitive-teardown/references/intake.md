# Intake

Ask for these before any rivalry draft. Record the founder's words. Mark a missing answer `[DECISION NEEDED]`.

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Offer | What a buyer gets and what they pay with |
| Geography | Where the founder will sell during the period under study |
| Named rivals | Companies, products, or people the founder already treats as competition |

Copy a price, a quote, or a URL when the founder already has it. Do not invent a rival to fill a blank cell; ask, then map substitutes and doing nothing from `map.md`.

Do not fill a field from a guess. If the founder names a person, a company, or a number, copy it as a fact and put it on the evidence table. If they describe a hope, label it an assumption.

Stay on founder planning. If the conversation turns into code, a voice rewrite, a skill pack, an engineering strategy, or laboratory work, stop this skill.
