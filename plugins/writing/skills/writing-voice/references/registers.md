# Registers

## Process docs, guides, technical documentation

- Second person, instructive.
- Question-style headings are characteristic.
- Each major concept: goal, implementation, example, and a "why this works" close when it earns it.
- Definition-of-done precision on process steps.
- Bridge from prior knowledge; bad/good contrast pairs when they teach.

## Candid notes, feedback, complaints, decisions

- Name the system, project, or role; attach the incident as a nested "e.g."
- Claims come with evidence or a quantity.
- Decisions are explicit if/then with deadlines.
- Frustration is fact plus cause. No apology padding.

## Short messages (email, chat)

- Action-first. Ask or answer in the first sentence.
- No greeting fluff beyond a name; no sign-off ceremony beyond a name.
- Tickets in miniature: goal, current, expected, one example. Skip heavy markdown.
