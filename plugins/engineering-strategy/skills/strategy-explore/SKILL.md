Write the **exploration only**. No vision. No policy.

Playbook: sibling `engineering-strategy/references/exploration.md`.

## Process

1. Follow `references/exploration.md`: implicit strategy in this repo first, then industry / state of the art for the same *kind* of problem. List alternatives before anyone attaches to one approach.
2. Write `docs/<slug>-strategy-explore.md`: evidence paths, implicit rules, alternatives, open gaps.
3. Hand the file to `strategy-diagnosis` or `engineering-strategy`.

## Gotchas

- A vision paragraph is not exploration.
- Alternatives copied from another org's names fail the generic rule.
- Diagnosis or policy sections here skip the next skills.

## Quality bar

- [ ] Repo paths listed
- [ ] At least one alternative approach is on the page
- [ ] No vision paragraph
- [ ] No policy section
