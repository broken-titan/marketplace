# Diagnosis

A **theory of the challenge** — root causes, not a complaint list.

Strategy fails without this section. Policies come after. A list of pains with no theory is unfinished.

## Build it

1. **Braindump** the circumstances that force a strategy now. Set it aside.
2. **Pull from exploration** every constraint and implicit rule that still holds. Tag each as fits / needs adjust.
3. **Mine disagreeing perspectives.** Talk to (or record from notes) people who will reject the early read. Goal is to *represent* their view, not to convert it.
4. **Synthesize** one internally consistent write-up that still states each view fairly. One author; do not crown a side.
5. **Test the draft** with the people expected to disagree until they agree their view is captured (they may still reject others’ views).

Directionally correct beats premature precision. Perfecting one camp’s numbers too early anchors the rest.

A diagnosis that only restates the user’s preferred solution is a slogan. Rewrite until an opponent could recognize their own view.

## Data

When numbers exist (headcount, flakiness, lead time, error budget, survey), put a short reading in the diagnosis and link the source.

When they do not exist, write **Data: missing** and what a later measurement would change. Missing data is common; silence about the gap is not.

## Tone

State awkward realities as facts without blame. A blocker (“leadership changes priority weekly”) becomes a diagnosis the strategy must address (“progress must be visible on a short clock”).

Prior decisions that created today’s problem belong in the diagnosis when the evidence supports them — including the author’s own.

## Output (standalone or section)

For `strategy-diagnosis`, write `docs/<slug>-strategy-diagnosis.md` (or a `## Diagnosis` block the full skill can paste). Include:

- Theory (one short paragraph)
- Causes (bullets, each with evidence path or **missing**)
- Perspectives (named roles or teams, not villains)
- Data (present with link, or missing)
- What a good policy would have to survive

Status: **Draft** until the full strategy is signed, or until the user accepts this section as input.
