# Examples

## Walking sentence (use this shape)

They came to the river at dusk, when the water was loud after rain and the fords uncertain, and on the far bank the reeds stood black against a pale sky, and no light showed in the houses beyond.

Not:

They came to the river. It was dusk. The water was loud. The fords were uncertain. The reeds were black. There were houses. No light showed.

## Naming inside the action

Wrong: He was an older man. He looked tired. His name was Almaz Krole.

Right: Almaz Krole met them in the doorway, an older man worn with labour, and drew them in before the watch could speak.

## What to leave out

From notes such as "average sized," "there is a lot of construction," "we split boys and girls to lose the watch," "the picture was okay but not great," keep only what a hearer of the tale would remember. Construction is already in the renovations. The quality of a photograph matters only if the image later serves. Losing the watch may be one clause, not a scene.

## Contrast formula (still forbidden)

Wrong: It is not a ledger of rooms and rolls. It is the story of what they found.

Right: Some rooms they found empty, the beds still made. In others the wounded had been laid, and a few they could not bring away.

## Speech on the page

Wrong: He told them it was high time they had come and pulled them into the office to speak of ghosts.

Right:

‘About time you showed your faces,’ said Krole, and he shut the watchman out. ‘Come in. I’ll not talk in the passage.’

He sat them down among the dust-sheets.

‘Since the workmen came I have seen folk in the old dress,’ he said. ‘Old armour. A sailor more than once, with a holster such as they do not make now. Then I began to find things moved, and I am not forgetful. That was when they found the first body.’

Wrong (one mouth in three bodies):

‘We should examine the arch,’ said Faye.
‘Yes, we should examine the arch,’ said Dahlia.
‘We should examine the arch,’ said Axel.

Right:

‘Put your hand where mine is,’ said Faye. ‘That is a lock.’
‘If it is a lock, he lied, or he never walks his own house,’ said Dahlia.
Axel only said, ‘Watch the street.’

## Mending a doubled death

Notes say a captain was lost at sea, and later that he was killed in a tavern. Write the first as rumour and the second as his own word.

## Unearned should (still forbidden)

Wrong: They began at an inn that should not have been haunted.

Right: They took their first night at the inn by the Crossings. The sign creaked though there was little wind, and the tapster set their plates down without looking at them.
