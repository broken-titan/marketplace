# Public technique (register, not a legendarium)

High-fantasy *register* is the product. Distinctive names, plots, and
languages from a still-protected legendarium are not. Do not paste
copyrighted narrative, letters, or appendices into the session. Do not
close-paraphrase those texts as if they were a style sheet.

Use public technique pages for the craft:

- [Tolkien's prose style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolkien%27s_prose_style) — plain diction argued against the charge of needless complexity; parataxis and "and" at heightened turns; register that follows people and rank; inversion and cadence used sparingly.

From that public commentary, keep these working habits:

- Prefer plain modern English. Grandeur is modulation at a turn, not a
  costume word on every noun.
- Side-by-side clauses with "and" can carry a scene farther than a stack
  of short facts.
- Variation of English by people, place, rank, and schooling. Not
  stage-accent and not a tic list.
- Do not flatten every speaker into one contemporary voice.

If the user asks for a named still-protected setting, stay in this
register and use only names and facts they supplied. Do not fetch or
quote the books.
