# Recap mode (fidelity)

Default when rewriting a journal, session log, or recap.

This is a rewrite contract, not a license to improve the tale.

## Never-change list

Do not alter:

- Who was present
- Where it happened
- What was done
- Who lived and who died
- How it ended
- Names, including dual spellings already in the source (keep both if the source has both)
- Scene order
- Existing quotes (wording the source already gives as speech)

## Invention

- No invented speech. If the notes only summarise a talk, keep it as
  narration. Do not mint dialogue.
- No silent mends. If the notes contradict themselves, keep both as the
  source has them (rumour then later word, or both reports) and do not
  pick a winner unless the user says which stands.
- Do not add a look, a habit, a motive, a new villain, a new gate, or a
  victory or defeat that did not happen.

## Mechanical residue

Dice, initiative, hit points, "I roll," table talk, and similar residue
become story (a blow that lands, a miss, a pause while they listen) or
are omitted. Do not leave the mechanics on the page. Do not invent a
blow the residue does not support.

## Tense and POV lock

Keep the tense and person of the source, or of the profile if the
profile set them. Do not slide from past to present, or from they to I,
to make it prettier.

## Output

- Return the **whole piece**, not a sample paragraph.
- Close with a **fidelity attestation**: a short list that the
  never-change items were kept, that no speech was invented, that
  contradictions were not silently mended, and that mechanical residue
  was turned into story or dropped. If you had to omit a bullet because
  it does not act, say so in the attestation, not in the tale.
