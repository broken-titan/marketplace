# Examples

Use generic names. Do not copy a table's proper nouns into the skill.

## Walking sentence (use this shape)

They came to the river at dusk, when the water was loud after rain and the fords uncertain, and on the far bank the reeds stood black against a pale sky, and no light showed in the houses beyond.

Not:

They came to the river. It was dusk. The water was loud. The fords were uncertain. The reeds were black. There were houses. No light showed.

## Naming inside the action

Wrong: He was an older man. He looked tired. His name was the innkeeper.

Right: The innkeeper met them in the doorway, an older man worn with labour, and drew them in before the watch could speak.

## Walking, not a tally

Wrong (tally):

The door flew wide. An older man drove a watchman out. He was of common height. His right hand twitched. Construction stood all about him.

Right (walking):

The door flew wide, and an older man, worn with years of work and with a twitch in his right hand, drove a watchman into the street.

‘About time,’ he said, noticing the assembled on his doorstep. ‘I asked for you days ago.’

This was the innkeeper, and he drew them with a motion through the dust of the renovations into his office.

(The quoted lines are for the original / NPC overlay. In recap mode, if the notes did not give those words, keep the greeting as narration.)

## What to leave out

From notes such as "average sized," "there is a lot of construction," "we split to lose the watch," "the picture was okay but not great," keep only what a hearer of the tale would remember. Construction is already in the renovations. The quality of a photograph matters only if the image later serves. Losing the watch may be one clause, not a scene.

## Contrast formula (still forbidden)

Wrong: It is not a ledger of rooms and rolls. It is the story of what they found.

Right: Some rooms they found empty, the beds still made. In others the wounded had been laid, and a few they could not bring away.

## Speech on the page

Wrong: He told them it was high time they had come and pulled them into the office to speak of ghosts.

Right (original / NPC only, and only if the notes or the user allow speech):

‘About time you showed your faces,’ said the innkeeper, and he shut the watchman out. ‘Come in. I’ll not talk in the passage.’

He sat them down among the dust-sheets.

‘Since the workmen came I have seen folk in the old dress,’ he said. ‘Old armour. A sailor more than once, with a holster such as they do not make now. Then I began to find things moved, and I am not forgetful. That was when they found the first body.’

If people, rank, and schooling are missing, ask. Do not ask for a pile of tics.

Wrong (one mouth in three bodies):

‘We should examine the arch,’ said the fighter.
‘Yes, we should examine the arch,’ said the scholar.
‘We should examine the arch,’ said the lookout.

Right:

‘Put your hand where mine is,’ said the fighter. ‘That is a lock.’
‘If it is a lock, he lied, or he never walks his own house,’ said the scholar.
The lookout only said, ‘Watch the street.’

A card such as "the fighter is short in talk" maps to terse familiar English.
"The scholar is church-trained and pleased with a phrase" maps to a more formal line.

## Unearned should (still forbidden)

Wrong: They began at an inn that should not have been haunted.

Right: They took their first night at the inn by the Crossings. The sign creaked though there was little wind, and the tapster set their plates down without looking at them.
