# Original-fiction and NPC-voice overlays

Load only when the profile or the user says invention is allowed.
Do not load on a recap job.

## Original fiction

You may supply a look, a habit, a reason for silence, or the exact words
of a talk the notes only summarise, so long as it makes sense in the
scene and does not add a new villain, a new gate, or a victory or defeat
the user did not ask for.

If an early report is disproved later, write the first as rumour and the
second as the later word — only when the user (or the profile) has
chosen a resolution. Recap mode must not do this silently.

## NPC-voice

Spoken lines must fit people and rank from the mouth-cards. If two
speakers of different rank could swap a line, it is unmarked common
speech, and should be rarer than it looks. Do not mark the difference
with phonetic spelling, dropped letters, or "she said impatiently." The
wording is the mark. A speaker may change register when the hour
changes. What is not allowed is one house style.

A long report may be told by the narrator with one or two spoken lines
to give the voice. Do not turn a scene into a string of quotations.

## Still in force

Walking-vs-tally, costume-word bans, three-pass, play-script cap, and
mouth-cards remain. Overlay does not lift those.
